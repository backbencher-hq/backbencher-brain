groovy: the arm's merge, the census's merge, and the spec writer that fills the spec from them

THE ARM IS MEASURED IN FIVE DISJOINT SLICES, and the two merges are the lane's own:
`script/m14/groovy-arm-merge.ts` sums the incoming-gate's own TABLE 1 over the slices and CHECKS
the closure to add up exactly, and `script/m14/groovy-census-merge.ts` sums the lane's census
(counts SUM, the trait-name sets UNION) over the same slices. The slices are slices of the GATE'S
OWN SORTED ADMITTED LIST, so they are disjoint and cover the population exactly — and BOTH merges
CHECK that claim from the instrument's own printed bounds rather than asserting it: the slices must
abut, start at 0, and reach the admitted total the instrument itself reports.

THE BIAS IS STATED WITH THE NUMBERS, NOT BOUNDED BY ASSERTION. The EDGE BUCKETS are the
population's: every admitted file is measured exactly once and an edge is a per-file fact. The
RESOLUTION BUCKETS are the chunked measurement's, and `resolved` is BIASED UP — the resolution
scope is PER PROJECT and a repository longer than one slice STRADDLES a slice boundary, so the
split half cannot see the collisions in the other half, and a collision that is INVISIBLE makes
the name read UNIQUE and the edge RESOLVE. The gate says so in its own population line, which
every slice prints. A number without its bias is a claim about nothing.

script/m14/groovy-spec-writer.ts WRITES the spec from the measurements rather than being
hand-maintained, so the spec cannot drift from the numbers the coverage-block gate arbitrates:
it reads the node-type census, the merged census and the merged closure, and it substitutes the
counts into the citation templates (script/m14/tmp/groovy-citations/*.txt) through
`{{PLACEHOLDER}}` slots. The three rulings' prose and the counts in them therefore come from ONE
source, and a stale count in the prose is impossible.

The lane's census grew the TRAIT-NAME output the mixes_in ruling needs (the names are a SET over
the population, so the merge unions them while the counts sum), and the small-slot runner
(script/m14/tmp/groovy-arm-run.sh) waits for the slot, runs the five slices of the gate and the
five slices of the census, and RELEASES — nothing is ever forced.
