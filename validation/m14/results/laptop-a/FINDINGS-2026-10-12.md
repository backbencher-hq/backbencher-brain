## 4. THE ZERO-RESOLUTION TYPES, AND WHAT THE ANALYZERS SAY

  `imports` — TS 2,337 (100%) · JS 1,324 (100%), AND NOT ONE AMBIGUOUS EDGE: a target that is
  ambiguous somewhere else would be ambiguous here. Mechanism, read at
  `real-typescript-analyzer.ts:5757` and its `:5708`/`:5737` siblings: the analyzer writes
  **`target: `${modulePath}:${itemName}``** (or `${modulePath}:*` for a side-effect import) —
  `react:useState`, `./OwnerType:OwnerTypeFromJSON`. That spelling is a MODULE-SPECIFIER JOINED TO
  AN ITEM NAME, and no declaration can ever be keyed by it, so the resolver's zero is structural
  rather than a collision. The metadata is rich (`module`, `alias`, `isTypeOnly`, `isSideEffectImport`,
  `isRequire`), so the information to act on is present; the TARGET is what cannot be matched.

  `overrides` — JS 132 (100%) in the stride, and **0 resolved of 420 in the whole-repo control**.
  Mechanism, read at `real-javascript-analyzer.ts:1540-1655`: the in-file branch checks
  `inFileDeclares` and emits `reasonCode: "verified-in-file"`; the CROSS-FILE branch emits
  **`reasonCode: "first-parent-guess"` at confidence 0.5, `evidenceClass: inferred`, WITHOUT
  CHECKING THAT THE BASE DECLARES THE MEMBER.** Measured on `bootstrap/js/src/base-component.js`:
  the analyzer publishes `BaseComponent` and `BaseComponent.dispose` correctly (12 symbols, alias
  present), and emits `BaseComponent.dispose -> Config.dispose` — but `Config`
  (`bootstrap/js/src/util/config.js`, 8 symbols) declares `_getConfig`, `_configAfterMerge`,
  `_mergeConfigObj`, `_typeCheckConfig` and **no `dispose` at all**. The target names a member the
  base does not have, so it can never resolve; the 84 ambiguous ones are the cases where a
  namesake exists elsewhere (`Config._getConfig` — 3 declarations).
  THIS IS NOT "EMITTED AND UNRESOLVABLE BY ACCIDENT" — the guess is deliberate and the verification
  exists one branch away. TS emits only 5 such edges (2 resolved), so the volume is a JS property.

  Also zero-or-near-zero, named for completeness: TS `implements` 1 edge (0 resolved) · JS
  `references` 9 of 195 (95.4% unresolved) · TS `extends` 9 of 48.
